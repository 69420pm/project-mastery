# How should the app show a low readiness score so it motivates instead of raising anxiety?

**Decision:** The wording, granularity and next-step framing of the topic-by-topic readiness shown before the exam.

**Why it's uncertain:** The vision promises honest readiness and less exam anxiety. A low score shown bluntly could paralyze, and one softened too much would not be honest.

## Sub-questions

- How does feedback about low performance affect test anxiety and effort?
- Which framings (actionable next steps, growth over time, comparison to goal) help?
- Do test-anxiety interventions such as expressive writing or reappraisal transfer to an app?

## What would change the design

Decides how readiness is displayed and what accompanies a low score.

Not researched yet.
