# How should the AI tutor ask, hint and explain so that the student does the thinking?

**Decision:** The tutor's dialogue policy: questions versus explanations, hint levels, and when to give the answer.

**Why it's uncertain:** Human tutoring effects are partly explained by how much the learner constructs (ICAP), and LLM tutors tend to over-explain.

## Sub-questions

- What makes human and intelligent tutoring effective?
- How should hint sequences be designed, and how do students abuse or avoid help?
- What do controlled studies of LLM tutors show about learning outcomes?

## What would change the design

Shapes the tutor's system prompt and hint levels.

## Outcome

Not researched yet.
