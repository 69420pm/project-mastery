# What feedback should the tutor give on a student's math work, and when?

**Decision:** Feedback type (correctness, hint, explanation of the error) and timing (each step, end of problem, delayed).

**Why it's uncertain:** Principle 2 wants feedback on the reasoning, not just the answer, but evidence on feedback timing and type is mixed.

## Sub-questions

- Is immediate or delayed feedback better for retention in problem solving?
- What does elaborated feedback add over correctness feedback?
- Does step-level feedback outperform answer-level feedback?

## What would change the design

Determines how the tutor reacts to each submitted step.

## Outcome

Not researched yet.
