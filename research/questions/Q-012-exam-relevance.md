# How can the app infer what matters for the exam from course materials and old exams?

**Decision:** The analysis that ranks topics by exam relevance, which feeds the plan and triage.

**Why it's uncertain:** This is more NLP and assessment design than learning science, and the evidence base is thin.

## Sub-questions

- How predictive are past exams of future exam content?
- How reliably can LLMs extract topics and map exam questions to them?

## What would change the design

Determines how much the plan should trust its relevance ranking versus the student's brief.

## Outcome

Not researched yet.
