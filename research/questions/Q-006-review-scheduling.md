# How should the app schedule reviews of each topic given the exam date?

**Decision:** The scheduling algorithm behind the plan: when each knowledge component comes back for review.

**Why it's uncertain:** Spaced-repetition algorithms like FSRS optimize long-term retention of flashcards, while the app targets a fixed exam date with problem-solving skills.

## Sub-questions

- What gap between reviews is optimal relative to the time until the exam?
- Do flashcard scheduling models transfer to problem-solving skills in math?
- How should reviews account for prerequisites between topics?

## What would change the design

Picks between an off-the-shelf algorithm, an exam-date-aware variant, or a simpler heuristic.
