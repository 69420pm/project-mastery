# How can the app estimate topic-by-topic readiness accurately from practice data?

**Decision:** The learner model behind readiness: which signals count and how they are combined.

**Why it's uncertain:** The vision's success metric is that the readiness estimate matches real exam results, and students' own judgments of learning are often overconfident.

## Sub-questions

- Which knowledge-tracing or IRT approaches predict exam performance best with sparse data?
- How do practice conditions (hints used, time since review) affect what a correct answer means?
- How should forgetting be modelled up to the exam date?

## What would change the design

Picks the readiness model and the data each session must record.

## Outcome

Not researched yet.
