# How can effortful practice still feel rewarding?

**Decision:** How sessions show progress and frame difficulty, so that desirable difficulties do not drive students away from the app.

**Why it's uncertain:** The vision promises less time and joy, but effective practice (retrieval, interleaving, spacing) feels slower and harder in the moment than rereading. Principle 7 rules out points and streaks for their own sake.

## Sub-questions

- Do desirable difficulties reduce motivation or persistence, and how much?
- Which ways of making progress visible sustain motivation without gamification?
- What does the research on 'aha' moments, curiosity and self-efficacy say about designing for the reward of understanding?

## What would change the design

Determines what the session UI shows after effortful practice, and whether difficulty needs to be explained to the student.

## Outcome

Not researched yet.
