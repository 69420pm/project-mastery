# What should a study plan look like when only days remain before the exam?

**Decision:** How the plan switches into triage: how topics are chosen and ordered, how much is retrieval versus new learning, and how reviews are spaced within a few days.

**Why it's uncertain:** Principle 3 defines triage as exam relevance × weakest mastery, but most spacing research targets long retention intervals, and the trade-off between covering new topics and consolidating known ones is unclear.

## Sub-questions

- What is the optimal gap between reviews when the retention interval is only days?
- With little time, is it better to cover more topics shallowly or fewer topics well?
- How much does sleep between sessions matter for short-horizon learning?

## What would change the design

The answer sets the triage algorithm: the number of passes per topic, the spacing gaps, and when to drop a topic entirely.

Not researched yet.
