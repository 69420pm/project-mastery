# Fan, Mihaylova, Akram, Norouzi, Brusilovsky, Hellas & Leinonen (2025). Adaptive learning curve analytics with LLM-KC identifiers for knowledge component refinement

ACM conference paper, 7 pages; venue not reported in OpenAlex. https://doi.org/10.1145/3754508.3754514

- **Design and population:** replication and extension of an LLM-based KC extraction method (LLM-KC Identifier) on an introductory programming course; number of students and assignments not reported in the abstract
- **Intervention vs. comparison:** KCs extracted by several LLMs vs. expert-annotated KCs
- **Outcome and delay:** overlap with expert KCs (Jaccard similarity, overlap coefficient) and learning-curve analysis of student error rates per KC; no learning outcome
- **Effect:** "certain LLMs (e.g., GPT-4o, DeepSeekR1) produce error-rate trajectories as smooth or smoother than expert-annotated KC models"; LLM KCs were identified consistently across assignments. No statistics in the abstract.
- **Boundary conditions:** the result holds for "certain LLMs", implying others did worse; not reported further
- **Quality notes:** short conference paper, abstract only (the full-text cap was already reached); "smooth learning curves" is a qualitative criterion unless quantified in the full text
- **Transfer notes:** university course and current models, and it tests KCs against student data, which is the criterion that matters; but programming, not math, and the KCs are tagged to assignments, not built from lecture material

> "certain LLMs (e.g., GPT-4o, DeepSeekR1) produce error-rate trajectories as smooth or smoother than expert-annotated KC models, effectively isolating student learning trends" (abstract)
