# Liu & Koedinger (2017). Closing the loop: Automated data-driven cognitive model discoveries lead to improved instruction and learning gains

Journal of Educational Data Mining, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3554625

- **Design and population:** a knowledge-component model improvement found automatically by Learning Factors Analysis was interpreted, validated on novel data, built into a classroom-deployed tutor and compared against a control condition; population to be read in the full text
- **Intervention vs. comparison:** tutor redesigned from the data-driven KC model vs. the original tutor
- **Outcome and delay:** learning gains; delay not reported in the abstract
- **Effect:** "significant learning gains relative to a control condition"; size not reported in the abstract
- **Boundary conditions:** not reported in the abstract
- **Quality notes:** one closing-the-loop study; data-driven discovery plus a controlled test is the strongest design in this literature
- **Transfer notes:** classroom tutor, likely secondary math (to confirm); shows that the KC model is a lever on learning, not only on prediction

> "show that the changes lead to significant learning gains relative to a control condition" (abstract)
