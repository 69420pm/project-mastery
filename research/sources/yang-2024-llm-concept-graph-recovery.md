# Yang, Yang, Ouyang, She, Feng, Jiang, Lecue, Lu & Li (2024). Leveraging large language models for concept graph recovery and question answering in NLP education

arXiv preprint. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2402.14293

- **Design and population:** zero-shot LLM recovery of a domain concept graph (prerequisite relations) for NLP education, evaluated against expert annotations; plus TutorQA, a 500-pair expert-verified benchmark
- **Intervention vs. comparison:** zero-shot LLMs vs. supervised graph methods
- **Outcome and delay:** F1 on edge recovery; no student outcome
- **Effect:** zero-shot concept graph recovery "competitive with supervised methods", with an average 3% F1 improvement; absolute F1 not reported in the abstract
- **Boundary conditions:** one domain (NLP), concepts given rather than extracted, to be confirmed in the full text
- **Quality notes:** preprint; benchmark built by the authors
- **Transfer notes:** university-level STEM-adjacent content; graph over a field, not over one course's materials

> "LLMs' zero-shot concept graph recovery is competitive with supervised methods, showing an average 3% F1 score improvement" (abstract)
