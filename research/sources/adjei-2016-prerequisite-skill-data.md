# Adjei, Botelho & Heffernan (2016). Predicting student performance on post-requisite skills using prerequisite skill data

Proceedings of the International Conference on Learning Analytics & Knowledge (LAK '16). https://doi.org/10.1145/2883851.2883867

- **Design and population:** student performance data from an online math practice system (ASSISTments, to confirm); population size not reported in the abstract
- **Intervention vs. comparison:** a data-driven measure of how strongly prerequisite skill performance predicts post-requisite performance, compared with a teacher survey of relationship strength
- **Outcome and delay:** agreement between data-driven and teacher-rated prerequisite strength
- **Effect:** "the survey results largely confirm our findings"; no statistic in the abstract
- **Boundary conditions:** distinguishes causal prerequisite links from non-causal co-occurrence; not reported further
- **Quality notes:** conference paper, abstract only; agreement claimed qualitatively
- **Transfer notes:** school math, not university; relevant as a method for checking prerequisite edges against practice data

> "we propose a simple, effective method of determining the strength of pre-to-post-requisite skill relationships" (abstract)
