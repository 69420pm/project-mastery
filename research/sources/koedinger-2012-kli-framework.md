# Koedinger, Corbett & Perfetti (2012). The Knowledge-Learning-Instruction framework: Bridging the science-practice chasm to enhance robust student learning

Cognitive Science, 36(5), 757–798. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1551-6709.2012.01245.x

- **Design and population:** theoretical framework drawing on research across science, math and language learning, much of it from the Pittsburgh Science of Learning Center (in vivo classroom experiments)
- **Intervention vs. comparison:** not applicable; proposes three coordinated taxonomies of knowledge, learning events and instructional events
- **Outcome and delay:** not applicable
- **Effect:** not applicable
- **Boundary conditions:** the abstract claims different learning processes lead to "different knowledge changes and constraints on optimal instructional choices"; details to be read in the full text
- **Quality notes:** highly cited (774 in OpenAlex) framework paper from the group behind the Cognitive Tutors and DataShop; theory, so it defines units and hypotheses rather than proving effects
- **Transfer notes:** built on secondary and college classroom tutors in math and science, close in material; not AI-mediated in the LLM sense

> "we identify three broad classes of learning events (LEs): (a) memory and fluency processes, (b) induction and refinement processes, and (c) understanding and sense-making processes" (abstract)
