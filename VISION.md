# Vision

> **The AI handles everything about learning except the learning itself.**

## Mission

Project Mastery helps students master their course material and excel in their exams: in less time than conventional studying, with understanding that lasts, and with the joy of things finally making sense. Students go into exams with less anxiety and more confidence in their own abilities.

## The problem

Students lose much of their study time to the bureaucracy of learning: finding what matters across slides, scripts and old exams, guessing what the examiner cares about, making a plan and sticking to it. Much of the remaining time goes into methods that feel productive but don't stick, such as rereading, highlighting and cramming. The strategies that work (spaced retrieval, interleaving, active problem solving) are well known, but hard to sustain alone against human nature.

This is what takes the fun out of studying: hours of effort with little visible progress, the feeling that everything slips away again, and growing anxiety as the exam gets closer. Studying isn't inherently boring. Inefficient studying is.

## How it works

1. **Upload**: The student uploads course materials: slides, scripts, exercises, old exams.
2. **Brief**: They tell the AI about the exam: date, format, what the lecturer emphasizes, what to skip, their goals and their current level.
3. **Plan**: The AI analyzes the materials and old exams, works out what matters most, and builds a learning plan that fits the student's time and preferences.
4. **Learn**: The AI tutors the student through the plan, session by session, and keeps adapting it to what the student has mastered, struggles with or wants.
5. **Ready**: Before the exam, the student practices on realistic mock exams and knows honestly how ready they are, topic by topic.

## Principles

1. **The student does the thinking.** The AI asks more than it tells: retrieval, problem solving, self-explanation. Explanations unlock the student's own work; they never replace it.
2. **Learning science by default.** Spaced retrieval, interleaving, worked examples that fade into independent practice, feedback on the reasoning and not just the final answer. The student never needs to know these terms; the app simply works this way.
3. **Long-term mastery wins; the exam date sets the mode.** With weeks to go, the plan builds durable knowledge, and the app supplies the consistency that is hard to keep up alone. With days to go, it switches to triage: highest exam relevance × weakest mastery first.
4. **Grounded in the course.** The uploaded materials are the primary source, cited down to the slide or page. Questions that go deeper or beyond them are welcome and get answered, clearly marked as going beyond the course.
5. **A tutor, not a supervisor.** The AI recommends what to do next. The student stays in control and can skip, reorder or redirect, and the plan adapts strongly to them.
6. **Honest, not paternalistic.** Solutions are not locked away. The AI encourages the student's own attempt first and helps step by step. If the student takes shortcuts, the app makes it visible that no learning happened.
7. **The reward is understanding.** Learning is fun when it works. Motivation comes from the moment a confusing topic becomes clear, from knowledge that stays, and from progress made visible. Points and streaks for their own sake are not the goal.
8. **Zero bureaucracy for the student.** Everything that isn't learning is the AI's job: finding content, analyzing old exams, planning, scheduling reviews.

## Who it's for

- **MVP:** university students in math and STEM subjects, preparing for written exams. English first.
- **Later:** more subjects and exam formats, K-12 students, and any language the AI handles well. Learning happens in the language of the student and their materials.

The MVP is built so it can expand in these directions, but none of this is built now.

## Non-goals

- **Never:** a homework or exam solver whose purpose is to skip the learning.
- **Not in the MVP:**
  - an open-ended general chatbot
  - note-taking or document management
  - tools for teachers or schools
  - social or collaborative features
  - users who are minors

## Built to be sustainable

Project Mastery is a showcase built to the standard of a real product: it must be able to serve real users and earn money without a redesign.

- AI usage is the main cost driver, so every feature is designed with its cost per student in mind. For example, each upload is processed once and reused, and each task uses the smallest model that does it well.
- No design decision closes the door on monetization. Accounts, usage metering and limits are first-class from the start.

## What success looks like

- Students learn more in less time than with conventional studying.
- Students walk into the exam calm and confident, knowing they are ready, and their grade confirms it.
- The app's readiness estimate matches real exam results.
- Students still remember the content weeks after the exam.
- Almost all time in the app is spent learning, not organizing.
- Students come back for their next exam because understanding felt good.

## Open questions

- Pricing model: what is free and what is paid.
- How students submit math work: typed, LaTeX, handwriting photo, step-by-step input.
- How readiness is measured, and how real exam results are collected to check it.
- Handling of uploaded course materials and student data: copyright, privacy, retention.
