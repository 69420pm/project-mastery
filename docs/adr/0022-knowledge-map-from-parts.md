# Knowledge map from Parts

**Context.** The first build sent every KC to most of its calls: at about 1,000 pages a dedup step's vectors passed Vercel Workflow's 50 MB step payload, one links call would answer for every KC, assignment and exercise prompts carried 46k to 64k tokens, and nothing was stored before the end, so a Daily limit below one build's cost restarted it every day (#222). It also stored judgment the tutor cannot trust: generated solutions, Bloom levels, typed relations, prior knowledge. Cheap models (Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna) need small prompts, and the AI already reads the chunks and pages in a Study session.

**Decision.** The map is built from Parts (#225), with bounded calls and the Materials' structure in code. Every call sees one Part, one exercise Part with its 30 nearest KCs, 15 clusters of alike KCs, 25 assumed names with 5 earlier candidates each, or up to 10 new Topics of one Big idea, so no prompt grows with the Course. Each step stores its results before it ends (`part_extractions` per Part, keyed by its `content_hash`), starts no call after 150 seconds and passes only ids, so a stopped build resumes without paying again and an unchanged Part makes no call. KCs carry a vector (`kcs.embedding`, matched by an exact scan in `match_kcs`) and merge into the oldest alike KC; a KC whose sources are gone is retired, keeps its Attempts and revives when taught again. Exercises keep only what is printed: final answers and the paired solution chunk, extracted again in place when their pair changes. Prerequisites come from the text inside a Part and from what a KC assumes, matched to earlier KCs; code breaks cycles and drops redundant links. Topics and Big ideas come from Parts and chapters in code: one Big idea per chapter title, a Part of 3 or more KCs is a Topic, a smaller one joins a Topic of its chapter, one over 10 KCs splits; a Topic keeps its id, name and central question while it holds Parts with the same content hashes (`topics.part_hashes`), and only new Topics get a naming call. This supersedes the map build of [ADR 0007](0007-ingest-each-upload-once.md) (chunks processed once through `knowledge_map_chunks`) and the Topics stage of [ADR 0020](0020-topics.md) (a model grouping KCs into Topics, with placements locked).

**Consequences.** Cost grows with pages, about $1.00 for a first build of 1,000 pages on those prices, estimated rather than measured. The map holds less: no Bloom level, Semantic link, prior KC, task type or generated solution, so the AI solves an Exercise without a printed solution with the Student and probes prior knowledge itself. Topics follow the Materials' headings, so a badly structured script gives uneven Topics, and a re-parse that changes a Part's content gives its Topic a new id, losing its Topic choices, exam scopes and notes. Duplicates the merge calls leave stay in the map and are listed in the check report.
