# Knowledge map from Chapters into a Topic tree

> The Prerequisite check: superseded by [ADR 0026](0026-assumptions-as-text.md), where what a KC assumes and no KC means stays as text on the KC instead of being dropped. The unit size, the retry rule, old exams and the Topic placement of KCs from the exercise call were changed by #249 and are marked below. The rest stands.

**Context.** The gold run of the pipeline v2 (#222, 2026-11-01) showed the Parts of [ADR 0022](0022-knowledge-map-from-parts.md) were too small: a Part of a few hundred words gave KCs without the context of their chapter, one call per page was slow on MiMo's 5 requests per minute, and exercise keys and partner Materials missed the numbering of real sheets. Big ideas, one per chapter title, flattened scripts whose chapters have sections, and an old exam bundled into a script could not count, because `is_exam` sat on the Material. An extraction with reasoning off misspelled Prerequisite names where the same prompt with reasoning on did not, for about 2,000 reasoning tokens a call (2026-11-01, `docs/agents/notes/ai-sdk.md`).

**Decision.** A parse reads pages in batches of up to 5 per call and finds each Material's **Outline** of Chapters in one structure call (#228), cutting chunks at Chapter starts; one pairing call per Course pairs exercises with solutions. The build (#229) extracts KCs per **Chapter unit**: a Chapter whose subtree's chunks of one kind total at most 25,000 characters (`UNIT_CHARS`, about 2 minutes of a call on MiMo; first 120,000, which no call finished inside a Vercel function) is one unit, a bigger one splits into its children and its own chunks, and consecutive children under the cap are packed into one unit. `chapter_extractions`, keyed by each unit's content hash, replaces `part_extractions`, so an unchanged unit makes no call. Every structured build call has reasoning on and ends after `CALL_TIMEOUT_MS` (170 s); a step starts no call that could end after 290 s. A call that times out or answers invalid leaves its unit to the next step, and a second failure fails the build with a reason, without a throw. KCs have no type and no budget. One `topic` call per build, made only when the Outlines or the names of KCs from teaching units changed, proposes a nested **Topic tree** over every Material's Outline; code places each Chapter with KCs in exactly one Topic and each KC from a teaching unit in its Chapter's Topic, a KC from an exercise call in the Topic of its tested KCs, of its nearest KC, or of its Chapter, keeps the ids of continued Topics and drops empty ones. Big ideas go: a Topic can hold Topics and KCs of its own, an exam scope sits on any Topic and is inherited down the tree. Old exams are Chapters the Student confirms in the Brief (`chapters.is_exam`), a Chapter covering its subtree, and the Reserved exam is a Chapter. An old exam's content forms no teaching units, so no KC is taught from it; its exercises still become Exercises. The Course language is the Student's choice in the Brief, else the Materials' most common one. Consolidation, the Prerequisite check and the exercise extraction stay as ADR 0022 decided. This supersedes ADR 0022 where they differ: Parts, Big ideas, KC types and budgets, the naming call and Topics kept by Part hashes.

**Consequences.** Calls grow with Chapters, not pages, and each prompt (up to about 7k tokens of chunks) costs reasoning tokens; whether 25,000 characters finish in 2 minutes on MiMo is measured by the gold run. Topic identity rests on the tree call continuing stored ids, not on content hashes. When the call forgets one, code continues the stored Topic by the new Topic that holds most of its Chapters, so a model that forgets a `continues` no longer loses a Topic's notes and choices unless no new Topic holds its Chapters. The tree call itself is made twice and, if it fails both times, every Chapter with KCs becomes a Topic. A Topic covers whole Chapters, so one Chapter is never split across Topics. A badly structured Material still gives an uneven tree, but the Outline falls back to the PDF outline when the structure call fails.
