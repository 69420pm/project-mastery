# Knowledge map from Chapters into a Topic tree

**Context.** The gold run of the pipeline v2 (#222, 2026-11-01) showed the Parts of [ADR 0022](0022-knowledge-map-from-parts.md) were too small: a Part of a few hundred words gave KCs without the context of their chapter, one call per page was slow on MiMo's 5 requests per minute, and exercise keys and partner Materials missed the numbering of real sheets. Big ideas, one per chapter title, flattened scripts whose chapters have sections, and an old exam bundled into a script could not count, because `is_exam` sat on the Material. An extraction with reasoning off misspelled Prerequisite names where the same prompt with reasoning on did not, for about 2,000 reasoning tokens a call (2026-11-01, `docs/agents/notes/ai-sdk.md`).

**Decision.** A parse reads pages in batches of up to 5 per call and finds each Material's **Outline** of Chapters in one structure call (#228), cutting chunks at Chapter starts; one pairing call per Course pairs exercises with solutions. The build (#229) extracts KCs per **Chapter unit**: a Chapter whose subtree's chunks of one kind total at most 120,000 characters is one unit, a bigger one splits into its children and its own chunks. `chapter_extractions`, keyed by each unit's content hash, replaces `part_extractions`, so an unchanged unit makes no call. Every structured build call has reasoning on, and one that answers empty or invalid is made once more. KCs have no type and no budget. One `topic` call per build, made only when the Outlines or KC names changed, proposes a nested **Topic tree** over every Material's Outline; code places each Chapter with KCs in exactly one Topic and each KC in its Chapter's Topic, keeps the ids of continued Topics and drops empty ones. Big ideas go: a Topic can hold Topics and KCs of its own, an exam scope sits on any Topic and is inherited down the tree. Old exams are Chapters the Student confirms in the Brief (`chapters.is_exam`), a Chapter covering its subtree, and the Reserved exam is a Chapter. The Course language is the Student's choice in the Brief, else the Materials' most common one. Consolidation, the Prerequisite check and the exercise extraction stay as ADR 0022 decided. This supersedes ADR 0022 where they differ: Parts, Big ideas, KC types and budgets, the naming call and Topics kept by Part hashes.

**Consequences.** Calls grow with Chapters, not pages, and fewer, larger prompts (up to about 30k tokens) cost reasoning tokens; the cap is unmeasured on paid models. Topic identity rests on the tree call continuing stored ids, not on content hashes, so a model that forgets a `continues` loses a Topic's notes and choices. A Topic covers whole Chapters, so one Chapter is never split across Topics. A badly structured Material still gives an uneven tree, but the Outline falls back to the PDF outline when the structure call fails.
