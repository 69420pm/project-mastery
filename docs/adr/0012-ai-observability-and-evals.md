# Observability and quality of AI behavior

**Context.** The AI's most important behavior is pedagogical: asking before telling, grounding answers in the course. An AI that starts handing out solutions breaks the first principle of [VISION.md](../../VISION.md), and no unit test catches that.

**Decision.** Trace every real AI call with Langfuse, not the mock provider's. Until the app runs on paid models, its AI quality is checked by hand: the maintainer uses the app on the gold materials in `evals/gold/` and logs findings with their Langfuse traces, and smoke checks (`pnpm smoke`) check single feature paths. There are no eval datasets or LLM judges. Report errors to Sentry, product analytics to PostHog, and send auth emails through Resend, since Supabase's built-in email is only meant for testing.

**Consequences.** Evals with LLM judges were tried first and removed on 2026-10-28 (tag `evals-v1`): on the free credit's models, a run took up to an hour at 5 requests per minute, and flash-class judges were not trusted enough to act on. Evals come back once a paid model runs the app and a frontier model judges, built from the logged findings and traces. Until then a prompt or model change is checked by its smoke check and by hand. Langfuse's free plan bills traces, observations and scores (50,000 a month), and mock-provider runs of the app had used most of it by mid-October 2026, so they are not traced. Only the model and tool spans are exported, not the AI SDK's `invoke_agent` and step spans around them, which cuts the units per call from 4 to 2. A call's cost in Langfuse is the one AI Gateway reports with the answer (`providerMetadata.gateway.cost`), cache reads included; Langfuse files cache reads under metadata and would otherwise price them as fresh input. Calls without one (the Gemini API locally) are priced from `src/lib/ai/prices.ts`, which `pnpm langfuse:models` pushes to Langfuse so it knows models such as MiMo. Prompts stay in code, where PR review covers them, and are not managed in Langfuse.
