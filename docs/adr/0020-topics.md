# Topics

> How Topics are made: superseded by [ADR 0022](0022-knowledge-map-from-parts.md), which builds them from Parts in code, keeps a Topic's id by its Parts' content hashes and names only new Topics. Stored Topics, their order and the anchors and Reading sections derived on read are unchanged.

**Context.** Learn sessions teach one Topic at a time (#170), so a Course needs Topics: units of a few KCs of one Big idea with a central question, that keep their identity as the map grows. Study sessions already point at a `topic_id` (ADR 0018), and later modes store notes and progress against it. Regrouping every KC on each build would give Topics new ids and names each time, and the Student's work would lose its anchor.

**Decision.** Topics are stored (#175): `topics` (name, central question, `position` in Course order, Big idea) and `topic_kcs` (a KC sits in at most one Topic, `position` in teaching order), owner-readable, written only by the build. Every build ended with a stage that grouped the KCs no Topic held, one `topic` call per Big idea with such a KC, so an unchanged Big idea cost nothing.

- **Code wins on an update.** KCs already placed stayed in their Topic and stored Topics kept their name and question; only the new KCs' placement and the model's new Topics were taken. Ids therefore survive updates.
- **Order.** KCs in a Topic are stably re-sorted so Prerequisites come first. Topics are ordered by Prerequisites between their KCs, then by earliest first source in Course order; a cycle breaks at the Topic with the earliest first source. Priority is not stored, so it applies on read.
- **Anchors and sections derived.** A Topic's anchors (Prerequisites of its KCs from outside it, in map order) are computed on read by `loadCourseTopics` from the map. So are its Reading sections (#224): the chunks its KCs cite, each once, in reading order, cut when the Material changes or a section would pass 3 pages. A section's id is a UUID v5 of its Topic id, Material id and pages (`node:crypto`), so a Done mark (`chat_messages.reading_section_id`) holds while the cut stays the same. The Student reads the pages as they are, and the tutor explains a hard page in the Chat. This supersedes the sections stored by a build stage in #178, which are gone.

**Consequences.** A build is `building` until its Topics are stored. Anchors and Reading sections follow a changed map or a re-parse on the next read, at the cost of reading the map and the Course's chunk places on each call, and a Done mark on a section whose pages changed no longer matches. Topic quality is checked by hand on the gold course, since there are no evals (ADR 0012).
