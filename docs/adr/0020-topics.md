# Topics

**Context.** Learn sessions teach one Topic at a time (#170), so a Course needs Topics: units of a few KCs of one Big idea with a central question, that keep their identity as the map grows. Study sessions already point at a `topic_id` (ADR 0018), and later modes store notes and progress against it. Regrouping every KC on each build would give Topics new ids and names each time, and the Student's work would lose its anchor.

**Decision.** Topics are stored (#175): `topics` (name, central question, why it matters, `position` in Course order, Big idea) and `topic_kcs` (a KC sits in at most one Topic, `position` in teaching order), owner-readable, written only by `replace_topics`, which also marks the build `built`. Every build ends with stage 2b: one `topic` call per Big idea with a non-prior KC that no Topic holds, so an unchanged Big idea costs nothing.

- **Code wins on an update.** KCs already placed stay in their Topic and stored Topics keep their name and question; only the new KCs' placement and the model's new Topics are taken, after a code check that every new KC appears exactly once. A KC removed from the map or moved to another Big idea leaves its Topic. Ids therefore survive updates.
- **Merge.** A Topic under 2 KCs merges into the Topic of its Big idea that shares the most Prerequisites and Semantic links with it, then the nearest first source; the absorbing Topic keeps its id. A Big idea's only Topic stays whatever its size.
- **Order.** KCs in a Topic keep the model's order, stably re-sorted so Prerequisites come first. Topics are ordered by Prerequisites between their KCs, then by earliest first source in Material order; a cycle breaks at the Topic with the earliest first source. Priority is not stored, so M3c can apply it on read.
- **Anchors derived, sections stored.** A Topic's anchors (Prerequisites of its KCs from outside it, prior KCs included, in map order) are computed on read by `loadCourseTopics` from the map. Its Reading sections (the chunks its KCs cite, in Material order, cut when the Material changes or a section would pass 3 pages) are cut by the build's stage 2c (M4a, #178) and stored in `reading_sections` with their mode; `loadCourseTopics` reads the stored rows, and a re-parse leaves them as they are until the next build. A stored section of the same Topic with the same chunks keeps its row across builds.

Prior KCs belong to no Topic. A failed Topics stage fails the build with a reason while the stored map stays, and the next Update map runs the stage again.

**Consequences.** `apply_knowledge_map_update` no longer marks the build `built`, so a build is `building` until its Topics and then its Reading sections are stored (`replace_reading_sections` marks it built), and Courses with a built map get Topics and sections on their next Update map. Anchors follow a changed map on the next read, at the cost of reading the map on each call. A model that groups poorly is fixed only for new KCs, since stored placement is locked; the Student's remedy is a rebuild of the Course. A `topics` judge eval for coherence and central questions is deferred, so those criteria are read by hand in `pnpm smoke topics`.
