# KCs without sources

> Superseded by [ADR 0026](0026-assumptions-as-text.md), where what a KC assumes and no KC means stays as text on the assuming KC, and every KC has sources.

**Context.** The Prerequisite check of [ADR 0022](0022-knowledge-map-from-parts.md), kept by [ADR 0023](0023-knowledge-map-from-chapters.md), turned a name a KC `assumes` into a Prerequisite only when it matched an earlier KC, and dropped it otherwise. So everything the Course's first KCs assume, from earlier semesters or other courses, was lost: the map had no place for it, and new knowledge had nothing to dock onto in the first Study session (#236).

**Decision.** What the Materials assume but never explain becomes an ordinary KC that has no rows in `kc_sources`: no new column, `origin` value, state or table. The extraction gives each assumed thing a name and a one-line description. The check embeds it as "name: description" and offers it the 5 nearest live KCs other than the assuming KC, wherever they sit in the Course; a KC without sources takes the place of the first KC that builds on it. An earlier KC becomes a Prerequisite as before, a later one nothing (a forward link could make the cycle check drop real Prerequisites), and no match, or no candidates at all, a new KC without sources that the assuming KC builds on. Such a KC revives a retired KC as alike as consolidation asks, and the new KCs of one step are made unique by name and through the merge call. `store_assumed_prerequisites` writes them, their links and the matched marks in one transaction. `retire_unsourced_kcs` retires a KC without sources only when no live KC builds on it, repeating until nothing changes. The Topic tree places a KC without sources in the Topic of the first KC, in Course order, that builds on it (directly or through other KCs without sources), directly before it; it stays out of the tree call. A later Material that teaches the concept gives it sources through consolidation's merge, with no extra code. This supersedes ADR 0023's "the Prerequisite check stays as ADR 0022 decided".

**Consequences.** The map now holds what the Course assumes, so it counts in Topic state, the study plan and Readiness like any KC, and the AI teaches it in the Chat from the Course's notation instead of a Reading section. A KC whose Material was deleted stays alive, now as assumed, while another KC builds on it. The number of KCs without sources per Course is unmeasured; a cap is deferred until a real build is read. The first Update map after this change extracts every teaching unit again, one call per unit.
