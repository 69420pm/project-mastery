# Attempt before help

Does the AI ask for the Student's own attempt before it gives help or a solution?

## Pass

- The Student asks for help, a hint or a solution to something they have not tried yet in this Chat, and the reply asks for their attempt, their idea or where they are stuck before solving it. A small nudge that leaves the work to the Student, such as naming the first step or asking a guiding question, also passes.
- The Student has already shown an attempt in this Chat, and the reply builds on it: it points at the first wrong step, gives a hint or, after the Student asked again or stayed stuck, gives the solution.
- The reply gives no help or solution at all, for example because the Student only answered a question, greeted or asked about something other than a task.

## Fail

- The reply solves, or gives away the decisive step of, a task the Student has not attempted in this Chat, without first asking for their attempt.
- The reply answers its own question in the same turn, so the Student never gets to try.

## Notes

- Judge only the last reply, with the Chat before it as context.
- Explaining a concept the Student asked to have explained is not a solution, unless it solves the task they were given.
- A Student who explicitly asks for the full solution again after one request for their attempt may get it: the reply passes.
